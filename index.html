Hide your name in the glass.

Wipe the mist off the glass, then slide it until the sunlight focuses. Then type a name: your browser shapes a new pane so its light writes that name on the wall.

The glass engine isn’t part of this build yet, so the bench can’t run here. The picture above is the set it runs in.

Up to 14 letters, numbers, spaces and & . - ’. Short names are set wide; long names are condensed to fit the glass.

Your text stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded: the glass and the light it casts are computed by physics on your device.

Process

How a project runs

  1. 01 Listen

    We learn your goals, audience, content and constraints, and agree what success looks like.

  2. 02 Shape

    Sitemap, structure, type, art direction and motion, tested with real content.

  3. 03 Build

    Engineered to be fast, accessible and easy to maintain, and tested on real phones and slow networks.

  4. 04 Launch

    Launch, measure, fix what the numbers show, and keep improving.

FAQ

Questions

What do you design and build?

Websites and web apps for businesses and brands, from strategy and design to development, motion and sound. Section by section, the list is under Services.

How does a project start?

You tell us what you’re making. We ask questions, then send a short plan and a written quote.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. We give you a schedule after the first call.

What does it cost?

Every project is scoped, and you get a written quote before any work starts.

Will my site be this heavy?

No. Effects are chosen per project, and we set budgets for performance and accessibility before design starts. Most sites need far less than this one.

Is all this motion and sound accessible?

There’s no background music. Short interface sounds answer what you do, like pressing a button or wiping the glass in the game, and nothing plays while you just read or scroll. The sound icon in the header turns them off at any time, and we remember that choice. There’s a volume control in the menu. Motion follows your device’s reduced-motion setting, and there’s a Still mode switch in the header. Everything works without either.

Can we edit content ourselves?

Yes. We set up a CMS that fits your team.

What happens to the name I typed?

It stays in your browser. Nothing you type in the game is uploaded or logged.

Where does my message go?

It’s emailed to our studio inbox, info@innovagroup.co.ae, through FormSubmit, an email-forwarding service. We reply by email.
