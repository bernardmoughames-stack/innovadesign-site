Privacy

What this site stores

Very little, and only in your browser. There are no cookies and no analytics.

In your browser

The site keeps your preferences in your browser’s local storage, on this device only. It never leaves your browser.

Keys this site may write
KeyWhat it holds
plainglass.prefs.v1Your Still mode choice, written only when you switch Still mode.
soundWhether interface sounds are on or off, so a choice to mute is remembered on your next visit.
sound-volumeThe volume you set in the menu.

What the page sends

Nothing you type is sent anywhere, except a contact message you choose to send.

The name you type in the game stays in your browser. The glass is computed on your device.

Contact messages

When you press Send, the contact form sends what you entered (your name, email address, company, needs, timing, budget and message, plus your pane’s name and choices if you tick “Include my pane”) to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), an email-forwarding service, which emails it to our studio inbox, info@innovagroup.co.ae. FormSubmit handles the message under its own privacy policy.

If you would rather not use the form, email or call us directly.

The host

The site is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps standard request logs (IP address, browser, the page path and the time), as any web host does. The part of a link after the # is never sent to it.

Analytics

There is no analytics or tracking of any kind on this site.
